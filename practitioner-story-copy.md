# Suggested practitioner story card copy

Direct quotes are verbatim excerpts; suggested summaries are editorial copy, not practitioner quotations.

- **Cindy Flosi, DPT — direct quote:** “Maybe my condition didn’t get worse. Maybe my body was just responding to how I was feeling. And that is a powerful discovery.”

  Source: https://howwefeel.substack.com/p/how-we-feel-in-practice

  Source: https://howwefeel.substack.com/p/what-comes-next

  Source: https://howwefeel.substack.com/p/finding-the-patterns
- **Saba Kerendian — direct quote:** “It helps me feel seen and validated in a way that is impactful towards my mental health.”
  Source: https://howwefeel.substack.com/p/why-tell-anyone-how-you-feel
